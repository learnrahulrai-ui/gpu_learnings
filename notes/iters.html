iters: repetitions until the while loop is left

Topics: the picture, splitting work. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

For each middleman pair (cx, cy) the lab repeats a short calculation and counts the repetitions. It leaves the while loop when x2 + y2 goes above 4, which means the point (x, y) has got farther than 2 from (0, 0), or when the count reaches max_iters. The picture stores the count, iters.

The question here: is every place about the same amount of work, so that giving each worker the same number of places is fair?

Work it out

L01 Lab 1 settings, from your output: img_size = 256 and max_iters = 1000, so the picture has 256 x 256 = 65,536 places, and each place costs from 1 to 1000 repetitions. from: your output, Lab 1 starter code

L02 Real data, computed with the lab's own lines for every place: the iters add up to 16,274,722, the "CPU total" your machine printed. Of the 65,536 places, 15,902 have iters 1000. from: computation on the lab code, your output

L03 DO Average iters per place = 16,274,722 / 65,536 = ____ . Share of places at 1000 = ____ percent. Share of all repetitions done by those places = 15,902 x 1000 / 16,274,722 = ____ percent. from: L01, L02

CHECK 
average = 248.3
places at 1000: 15,902 / 65,536 = 24.3 percent
their repetitions: 15,902,000 / 16,274,722 = 97.7 percent of all the work

L04 Split the picture between 4 workers by i, in consecutive bands: i = 0 to 63, 64 to 127, 128 to 191, 192 to 255. Each band has 16,384 places. Sums of iters per band, from the same data: 462,567; 7,558,684; 7,747,009; 506,462. from: computation on the lab code

L05 DO Share of the work in the largest band = ____ percent. The job ends when the slowest worker ends, so if time follows repetitions, 4 workers are faster than 1 by 16,274,722 / ____ = ____ . from: L02, L04

CHECK 
largest band: 7,747,009 / 16,274,722 = 47.6 percent
speedup = 16,274,722 / 7,747,009 = 2.10, not 4

A quarter of the places do almost all the work (L03), and they sit together in the middle bands.
Equal numbers of places are not equal work; a split by count of places is uneven before it begins.
