The mask, and why all ones reads as -1

Topics: CPU. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

In the AVX2 while loop every instruction acts on all 8 indexes, so no single index can be told to stop. Lab 1's vector code instead keeps 8 counters in one variable and adds 1 only where the place is still inside the loop, using a compare and one subtraction.

The question here: why does subtracting the compare result add 1?

Work it out

L01 Your code: __m256 mask = _mm256_cmp_ps(_mm256_add_ps(x2_vec, y2_vec), four, _CMP_LE_OQ); In English: at each index, add x2 and y2 and compare the sum with 4; where it is at most 4 put 32 bits all 1, elsewhere 32 bits all 0, and keep the 8 answers in mask. from: your code, intrinsic definition

L02 DO Add 1, in binary, to the 32-bit integer whose bits are all 1. Every 1 + 1 gives 0 and carries 1 upward; the carry out of bit 31 does not fit. The 32 bits left = ____ . So all ones, read as a signed 32-bit integer, is ____ . from: L01

CHECK 
  11111111 11111111 11111111 11111111
+                                   1
= 1 00000000 00000000 00000000 00000000   (the leading 1 does not fit)
32 bits left: all 0, which is 0
the integer that becomes 0 when 1 is added is -1: all ones = -1

L03 Your code: iters_vec = _mm256_sub_epi32(iters_vec, _mm256_castps_si256(mask)); In English: read the bits of mask as 8 whole numbers, without changing any bit, and subtract them from the 8 counters, index by index. from: your code, intrinsic definition

L04 Fact about this loop: an index whose scalar iters is c has x2 + y2 at most 4 before each of repetitions 1 to c, and above 4 before repetition c + 1 and every later one; once (x, y) is farther than 2 from (0, 0), every further repetition keeps it farther. from: the lab's while loop, Mandelbrot iteration

L05 DO Index with all ones: iters - (____) = iters + ____ . Index with all zeros: iters - 0 = ____ . Real block i = 40, j = 208 to 215, scalar iters 12, 8, 7, 6, 6, 6, 5, 5: iters_vec after repetition 7 = ____ ; after repetition 12 = ____ . from: L02, L03, L04

CHECK 
all ones:  iters - (-1) = iters + 1
all zeros: iters - 0 = iters
after repetition 7:  7 7 7 6 6 6 5 5
after repetition 12: 12 8 7 6 6 6 5 5   = the scalar iters

No index was asked whether it had finished. Its own mask value stopped its own count.
