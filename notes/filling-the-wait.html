Filling the 4-cycle wait: needed against held

Topics: GPU chip. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

While one set of 32 threads waits for a result, a scheduler can begin an instruction for another set that is ready. The hardware can hold many sets per scheduler. How many a program needs depends on how long each wait is and how long each instruction takes to begin.

The question here: on this laptop, how many sets of 32 threads does each scheduler need for chains of multiply-adds, and how does that compare with what it can hold?

Work it out

L01 NVIDIA facts, GTX 1650 (Turing): 14 SMs. An SM handles threads in consecutive sets of 32, which NVIDIA calls warps. Each SM has 4 schedulers and holds at most 32 warps; each scheduler begins at most one warp's instruction per cycle. from: NVIDIA Turing Tuning Guide, CUDA programming guide

L02 Measured on your GTX 1650 in the Lab 2 prelab: a 32-thread FFMA takes 2 cycles to begin, and an FFMA that needs the previous one's value waits 4 cycles for it. from: your output

L03 DO Warps one scheduler holds = 32 / 4 = ____ . When every warp is a single chain of FFMAs, warps one scheduler needs = 4 / ____ = ____ . Whole GPU: needs 14 x 4 x ____ = ____ warps; holds 14 x 32 = ____ . from: L01, L02

CHECK 
holds 8 per scheduler
needs 4 / 2 = 2 per scheduler (warp A at cycle 0, warp B at cycle 2, warp A's next ready at cycle 4)
whole GPU: needs 112 warps, holds 448
The maximum the chip allows is a ceiling; the target comes from the wait and the begin time.

L04 DO The course GPU (RTX 4000 Ada, 48 SMs, 128 FP32 units per SM, so 32 per scheduler) begins a 32-thread FFMA in ____ cycle(s). With the same 4-cycle wait assumed, warps needed per scheduler = ____ . Your Lab 2 notes counted 224 warps for the GTX 1650 with that per-scheduler figure: 14 x 4 x ____ = 224. The count from L03 = ____ . from: L01, L02, NVIDIA specifications, your Lab 2 notes

CHECK 
32 threads / 32 units = 1 cycle
4 / 1 = 4 warps per scheduler
14 x 4 x 4 = 224: the course GPU's figure applied to the laptop
L03: 112. The laptop's 2-cycle begin halves the count.
