From the formula to threads and a loop

Topics: Lab 4, splitting work. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

A formula can be read in the order it is written, A first, then B, then C, or from the place it writes. A GPU kernel needs to know which parts of the work can be done at the same moment and which must meet in one place.

The question here: which indices of the matrix product become threads, which becomes a loop, and what cuts that loop into pieces?

Work it out

L01 The Lab 4 starter's CPU reference: c[i * size_j + j] is the sum over k of a[i * size_k + k] * b[k * size_j + j], with size_i = size_j = size_k = 3072 in its timed test. The Lab 4 handout: the course GPU's L2 cache delivers 2.4 TB/s, each of its SMs, the identical blocks of hardware the chip is built from, can give a block of threads at most 100 KB of shared memory, and the first kernel must finish the 3072 test in under 45 ms. from: the Lab 4 starter; the Lab 4 handout
