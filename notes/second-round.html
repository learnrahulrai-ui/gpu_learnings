A second round of blocks

Topics: GPU chip, splitting work. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

A launch can ask for more blocks of threads than the SMs can hold at once. The extra blocks wait until earlier blocks finish, then execute as a further round. A small increase in blocks can then add a whole round of time.

The question here: does that explain the jump in the course file warp_scheduler.cu between multiplier 8 and multiplier 9?

Work it out

L01 The course file warp_scheduler.cu launches 48 x m blocks of 64 threads; each block handles 2,048 numbers, and each number is updated 5,260 times by a multiply-add, counted as 2 operations. from: Lab 2 starter code, your Lab 2 notes

L02 Reported for the course GPU (RTX 4000 Ada, 48 SMs) in your Lab 2 notes: m = 8 gave 26.4575 x 10^12 operations per second; m = 9 gave 15.9401 x 10^12. from: your Lab 2 notes

L03 DO Operations per block = 2 x 2,048 x 5,260 = ____ . For m = 8: ____ blocks, ____ operations, time = operations / rate = ____ ms. For m = 9: ____ blocks, ____ operations, ____ ms. Work ratio = ____ . Time ratio = ____ . from: L01, L02

CHECK 
21,544,960 operations per block
m = 8: 384 blocks, 8,273,264,640 operations, 8,273,264,640 / 26.4575e12 = 0.3127 ms
m = 9: 432 blocks, 9,307,422,720 operations, 9,307,422,720 / 15.9401e12 = 0.5839 ms
work ratio 432 / 384 = 1.125, time ratio 0.5839 / 0.3127 = 1.867

L04 DO Hypothesis, not a measured fact: an SM holds at most 8 blocks of this kernel at once. Blocks held at once = 48 x 8 = ____ . For m = 9, blocks that wait = ____ . If the second round takes as long as the first: time = 2 x 0.3127 = ____ ms, against 0.5839 measured, a gap of ____ percent. from: L03

CHECK 
384 held; 432 - 384 = 48 wait for a second round
2 x 0.3127 = 0.6254 ms predicted, 0.5839 measured, 6.6 percent apart
Close but not equal: suggestive, not proof. The test is the number of blocks one SM holds
(cudaOccupancyMaxActiveBlocksPerMultiprocessor) and a finer sweep of m around 384 blocks.
