%0 and %1: binding variables to an instruction

Topics: Lab 6, GPU chip. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

An instruction that the kernel's language does not have is inserted as text with an asm statement, and the text never mentions a variable of the program. Something connects the two.

The question here: how does an asm statement say which variable stands for which operand, and which of them the instruction writes?

Work it out

L01 NVIDIA's guide Inline PTX Assembly in CUDA: asm("template-string" : "constraint"(output) : "constraint"(input)); each %n of the template refers to the n-th operand of the lists, counted in text order from 0, outputs first; "=" marks a register that is written; "r" is a 32-bit register, "f" a 32-bit float register. from: NVIDIA's guide Inline PTX Assembly in CUDA

L02 DO In asm("add.s32 %0, %2, %1;" : "=r"(i) : "r"(k), "r"(j)), what are %0, %1 and %2, and what does the line compute? ____ . from: L01

CHECK 
%0 = i, %1 = k, %2 = j: the instruction computes i = j + k

L03 DO The Lab 6 tensor core statement lists 4 outputs and 4 + 2 + 4 inputs. Which numbers belong to D, A, B and C? ____ . from: L01

CHECK 
%0 to %3 are D, %4 to %7 are A, %8 and %9 are B, %10 to %13 are C
