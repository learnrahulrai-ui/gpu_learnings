SM: the block of hardware a GPU chip is built from

Topics: GPU chip. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

An NVIDIA GPU chip is built from identical blocks of hardware that NVIDIA calls SMs. Each SM has its own arithmetic circuits, schedulers that decide which instruction begins, and registers. These are fixed by the chip and exist whether or not any program uses them.

The question here: does a chip with far more arithmetic circuits win by default?

Work it out

L01 GTX 1650 (Turing), fixed by the chip: 14 SMs; each SM has 64 FP32 units (circuits that each do one float multiply-add for one thread), 4 schedulers and 65,536 registers, and holds at most 1,024 threads at once. A launched block of threads executes on one SM. from: your hardware table, NVIDIA Turing Tuning Guide, CUDA programming guide

L02 Ryzen 5 4600H: 6 cores, each with AVX2 instructions that act on 8 floats at once. from: your hardware table

L03 DO FP32 units on the GPU = 14 x 64 = ____ . Floats the 6 cores act on with one AVX2 instruction each = ____ . Ratio = ____ . Threads the GPU holds at once = 14 x 1,024 = ____ . from: L01, L02

CHECK 
896 FP32 units (NVIDIA lists 896 CUDA cores for this GPU)
6 x 8 = 48 floats
896 / 48 = 18.7 times more float circuits
14 x 1,024 = 14,336 threads held at once

L04 Your Lab 1 times, 256 by 256 picture: CPU with AVX2 on one core 8.71 ms; GPU with one block of 32 threads 17.732 ms. from: your output

L05 DO Share of the CPU's cores the 8.71 ms used = 1 / 6 = ____ percent. Share of the GPU's FP32 units the 17.732 ms used, at most 32 / 896 = ____ percent. SMs that had work = ____ of 14. Does "the CPU finished first" show that the GPU is slower for this job? ____ . from: L01, L02, L03, L04

CHECK 
CPU: 1 / 6 = 16.7 percent of its cores
GPU: 32 / 896 = 3.6 percent of its FP32 units, at most
one block executes on one SM: 1 of 14 SMs had work, the other 13 had none

No. The GPU result used about 1/28 of its arithmetic circuits, the CPU result 1/6.
The comparison says how each launch used its chip, not what each chip can do.
