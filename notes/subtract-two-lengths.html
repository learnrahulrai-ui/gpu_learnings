Measuring by subtracting two lengths

Topics: measuring. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

Every timing contains a fixed part unrelated to the work being timed: the clock reads, setup, the wait at the end. Dividing one timing by the number of operations spreads that fixed part over the operations. Timing the same kernel at two lengths and subtracting cancels it.

The question here: how large is the fixed part on this GPU, and what wrong conclusion does a single division lead to?

Work it out

L01 Your GTX 1650, one chain of dependent multiply-adds (FFMA instructions, each needing the previous one's value): 64 FFMAs printed 281 cycles; 256 FFMAs (the copy-paste bug kernel) printed 1055 cycles. from: your output

L02 DO 281 / 64 = ____ . (1055 - 281) / (256 - 64) = ____ / ____ = ____ . From the first FFMA beginning to the 64th: 63 gaps of that size = ____ cycles. Part of the 281 outside the chain = ____ . from: L01

CHECK 
281 / 64 = 4.39
774 / 192 = 4.03 -> 4 cycles from one FFMA to the next in a chain
63 x 4 = 252 cycles
281 - 252 = 29 cycles outside the chain
4.39 is 4 plus those 29 cycles spread over 64.

L03 Same GPU, two independent chains side by side: 128 FFMAs in all printed 295 cycles, 256 printed 551. With four chains: 256 printed 519, 512 printed 1031. from: your output

L04 DO By subtraction: two chains (551 - 295) / (256 - 128) = ____ ; four chains (1031 - 519) / (512 - 256) = ____ . By single division: 295 / 128 = ____ ; 519 / 256 = ____ . Which method says four chains are faster than two, and by how much? ____ . from: L03

CHECK 
subtraction: 256 / 128 = 2 and 512 / 256 = 2: four chains gain nothing over two
single division: 2.30 and 2.03: four chains look 2.30 / 2.03 = 1.14 times faster
The single division invents a 14 percent gain: the fixed part is spread over more FFMAs.
