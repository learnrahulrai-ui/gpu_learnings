Independent chains inside one thread

Topics: GPU chip, chains. A single idea, with a few lines to calculate. Each line uses only the lines above it, and the grey note names its source. Fill every ____ on paper, then open CHECK. Any unfamiliar word: words used on this site.

The idea

A chain is a list of operations in which each needs the value of the one before it, so they cannot overlap. If a thread carries a second chain whose values never depend on the first, the GPU can begin an operation of the second chain while the first waits.

The question here: how many chains does it take before extra chains stop helping, and what sets that number?

Work it out

L01 Measured on your GTX 1650 by subtracting two lengths of each kernel: one chain 4 cycles per FFMA (1055 - 281 over 192 FFMAs), two chains 2 (551 - 295 over 128), four chains 2 (1031 - 519 over 256). from: your output

L02 Turing facts: an SM has 64 FP32 units and 4 schedulers, so each scheduler has 64 / 4 = 16 FP32 units. An FFMA instruction is for 32 threads, and a scheduler begins at most one instruction per cycle. from: NVIDIA Turing Tuning Guide

L03 DO Cycles to feed 32 threads through 16 FP32 units = ____ . In one chain an FFMA begins every 4 cycles (L01). FFMAs of other chains that fit into those 4 cycles = (4 - ____) / ____ = ____ . Chains that keep the FP32 units busy = ____ . Does it match L01? ____ . from: L01, L02

CHECK 
32 / 16 = 2 cycles per FFMA
cycle 0: chain A begins (units busy in cycles 0-1); cycles 2-3: one FFMA of chain B;
cycle 4: chain A's next FFMA
(4 - 2) / 2 = 1 FFMA of another chain fits -> 2 chains keep the units busy
matches: 4 cycles with one chain, 2 with two, still 2 with four

L04 DO Competing guess: "an FFMA can begin every cycle, so k chains give 4 / k cycles per FFMA, never below 1." For four chains it predicts ____ ; measured ____ . Which survives, the guess or L03? ____ . from: L01, L03

CHECK 
guess: 4 / 4 = 1 cycle; measured 2 -> the guess fails, L03's 2-cycle feed survives
With two chains both give 2, so only the four-chain measurement could tell them apart.
