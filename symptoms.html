Start from your symptom

You are in the middle of the lab and something does not add up. Find the sentence that sounds like what you are seeing, and go straight to the page for it. Every page restarts from zero, so you can enter anywhere.

Most people do not arrive at a site like this at the beginning. They arrive stuck, often holding code that works but that they could not have written alone and could not defend. Each page here opens with a "Guess first" box: write a number before reading. Each page closes by comparing your guess with what a real laptop measured, and with the question an interviewer would ask next. Keep a tally on paper, one line per page, marking whether your guess was close. The tally is yours; nobody else sees it.
