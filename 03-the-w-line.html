Step 1: expand one square long-hand

Line E makes z = x + y. Line F makes w = z * z = (x + y) * (x + y). Expand it as four products, no shortcut.

        (x + y) * (x + y)
       = x*x  +  x*y  +  y*x  +  y*y
                 \______  ______/
                        \/
                     ____ * x*y

w - x*x - y*y = 2*x*y

line B:  y = w - x2 - y2 + cy   is   y = 2*x*y + cy   in disguise.
